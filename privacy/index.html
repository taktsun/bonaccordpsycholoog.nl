Confidentiality and Privacy

Last updated on 2026-01-01

Service-related Confidentiality

Bon Accord Psycholoog is bound by the Code of Professional Ethics from the Nederlands Institute van Psychologen (Netherlands Institute of Psychologists), version 2024. Articles 62, 70, 74, 75 apply in relation to service-related confidentiality.

All information disclosed by clients is treated as confidential. Confidentiality may only be breached when:

  • In prevention of direct threats (e.g., a life-threatening risk) to the Client or other persons, or
  • The Psychologist is legally obligated to do so by statutory provisions or a court order.

If confidentiality must be breached, Bon Accord Psycholoog will inform the persons involved as long as not compromising the prevention of direct threats.

Website-level Privacy

We respect your privacy. This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics services.

Contact form: If you choose to contact us via the “Contact” page, we will collect the personal data you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

Server logs: This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered via Cloudflare. Both services may automatically collect and store server logs when you visit the site. These logs can include your IP address, browser type, operating system, and the date and time of your request. This information is collected for security, performance optimization, and technical troubleshooting, and is retained for a limited period in accordance with GitHub’s and Cloudflare’s privacy policies.

Data retention: We keep your message and contact details only as long as necessary to handle your request, unless legal obligations require longer storage.

Sharing of data: Your data provided to us will not be shared with other third parties.

Your rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us.
